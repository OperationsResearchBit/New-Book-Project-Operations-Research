# Operations Research & Enterprise Architecture

## 1. Lessons from World War Operations
How large‑scale coordination, logistics, and resource allocation during global conflict laid the foundation for modern OR.

### Topics include:
* **Early mathematical modeling**
* **Supply chain and convoy optimization**
* **Decision systems under uncertainty**
* **The birth of structured operational analysis**
